# Algothon 2026 Research Notes

Private, notes-only archive of our Algothon 2026 research process.

This repository records:

- major milestones;
- how the strategy ideas evolved;
- the validation and decision-making process;
- lessons that may transfer to future quantitative research.

It intentionally excludes:

- strategy source code or pseudocode that can be directly implemented;
- asset names, selected pairs, exact thresholds, and final parameters;
- price data and derived position or PnL series;
- submission packages, hashes, IDs, and portal receipts;
- local file paths or personal information.

## Contents

- [TIMELINE.md](TIMELINE.md) — chronological research milestones.
- [IDEAS.md](IDEAS.md) — evolution of the main strategy concepts.
- [METHODOLOGY.md](METHODOLOGY.md) — validation and evidence discipline.
- [LESSONS.md](LESSONS.md) — conclusions and reusable lessons.

## Status

This is an internal record, not a submission artifact and not an open-source
release. The repository is intended to remain private while competition and
publication permissions are unresolved.
